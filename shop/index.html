FAQs about TopPharma

Can I buy medicines online from TopPharma?

You can browse available medicines and healthcare products through the TopPharma online shop. Some products may have prescription or professional assessment requirements before they can be supplied.

Do all medicines require a prescription?

No. Prescription requirements depend on the individual medicine and its legal classification. Products requiring a prescription must go through the appropriate process before supply.

How can I find a specific medicine?

Use the shop search function or browse the relevant category. If you cannot locate a particular product, contact the TopPharma team for assistance.

Can I order prescription medicines online?

Prescription medicines may be ordered online where the appropriate prescription or clinical assessment process is available. They must not be supplied without the required authorisation.

How does the prescription process work?

Depending on the product, you may need to provide a valid prescription or complete a health assessment. The relevant information is reviewed before an eligible prescription medicine is supplied.

How should I use my medicine?

Always follow the instructions provided with your medicine and the advice of your doctor, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare professional. Do not change the dose or treatment duration without professional advice.

What if I cannot find the medicine I need?

Try searching using the medicine's name or browse the most relevant category. If you still cannot find it, contact TopPharma and ask whether the product is available.
