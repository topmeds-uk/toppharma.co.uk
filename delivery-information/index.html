Useful information

Ask your pharmacy about delivery.

Practical information from Top Pharma.

Speak to your dispensing pharmacy

Top Pharma does not dispatch medicines or arrange delivery. Contact the pharmacy handling your prescription for information specific to your order.

Useful things to check

  • When should you expect dispatch and delivery?
  • Does someone need to be available to receive the parcel?
  • Are there any special storage or handling instructions?
  • Who should you contact about a delay or damaged packaging?

Follow the advice supplied with your medicine and ask your pharmacist if anything is unclear.
