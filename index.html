09 / BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

A few useful answers.

What this website can help you find.

Is Top Pharma available throughout the UK?

The information on this website can be read across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. This website does not dispense or deliver medicines.

Do I need a prescription to order medicines?

Top Pharma does not accept medicine orders. Discuss prescription requirements and medicine suitability with a qualified healthcare professional.

How do I find a medicine on Top Pharma?

Use the medicine directory search or browse by category. Each medicine page links to an external patient information resource.

Can I contact someone about my medicine?

Speak to your doctor or pharmacist for personalised advice. The contact page explains where to find appropriate support.

How will my medicine be delivered?

Top Pharma does not arrange delivery. If another pharmacy dispenses your medicine, contact that pharmacy about its delivery arrangements.
