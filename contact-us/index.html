Contact & support

Contact TopPharma

Have a question about an order, medicine, delivery, or our online pharmacy service? The TopPharma team is here to help.

Whether you need an update on an existing order or simply want to ask a general question, you can contact us using the available support options below. We aim to make every enquiry as clear and straightforward as possible.

Get in Touch With TopPharma

Our customer support team can assist with general questions about our online pharmacy services, orders, and website.

How Can We Help?

For questions involving your personal health or treatment, you may need to provide relevant information so that your inquiry can be handled appropriately. You can contact TopPharma regarding:

  • Order status and order inquiries
  • Product availability
  • Delivery and shipping questions
  • Payment-related inquiries
  • General product information
  • Website or account support
  • Returns and refund inquiries
  • General customer service
  • Feedback or complaints

Send Us a Message

Use our online contact form to send your enquiry to the TopPharma support team. For an existing order, including your order number can help us identify your enquiry and respond more efficiently.

Please provide:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Subject
  • Your message

Customer Support Hours

Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Response times may vary depending on the nature of your enquiry and when we receive your message. We aim to respond as soon as reasonably possible during our customer support hours.

Questions About Your Order?

If you have already placed an order with TopPharma, please include your order reference when contacting us. This can help our team locate your order and provide more relevant information about its status, delivery, or other concerns.

For delivery enquiries, please provide any available tracking information along with your order reference.

Need Help With a Medicine?

For general medicine-related questions, you can contact our support team. Depending on the nature of your question, additional information may be required.

Please remember that general customer support should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have concerns about your health, treatment, dosage, side effects, or a medical emergency, seek advice from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional or the relevant UK emergency service.

Contact TopPharma Online

Our online contact service is designed to make it easier for customers to reach us without unnecessary hassle.

Whether you need help with an order, have a question about our pharmacy services, or want to provide feedback, we're ready to listen and help where we can.

FAQs

How can I contact TopPharma UK?

You can contact TopPharma through the contact form and other communication channels provided on our website. Choose the most suitable option for your inquiry and provide accurate details so our team can assist you.

Can I contact TopPharma about an existing order?

Yes. If you have an existing order, include your order number or reference along with your name and email address. This can help us find the relevant order more quickly.

Can I ask about product availability?

Yes. You can contact our team with questions about product availability. Availability may change depending on stock and applicable prescription or clinical requirements.

How long does TopPharma take to respond?

Response times can vary depending on the type of enquiry and when it is submitted. We aim to respond to customer inquiries as soon as reasonably possible during our stated support hours.

Can I use the contact form for urgent medical advice?

No. The contact form is intended for general customer and service inquiries. It should not be used for urgent medical concerns or emergencies. For urgent medical assistance, contact an appropriate UK healthcare or emergency service.

Can I contact TopPharma about delivery?

Yes. You can contact us regarding delivery status, shipping questions, or other delivery-related concerns. Including your order reference can help us assist you more efficiently.
