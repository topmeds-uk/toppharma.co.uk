Frequently Asked Questions About Top Pharma

Can I order prescription medicine from Top Pharma?

Eligible prescription medicines can only be supplied after the relevant prescription and pharmacy requirements have been completed. Requirements vary depending on the medicine and circumstances.

Does Top Pharma deliver throughout the UK?

Top Pharma is designed to serve customers across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland where delivery of the relevant product is permitted and available.

How can I check whether a medicine needs a prescription?

The prescription status should be clearly displayed on individual medicine pages. If you are unsure, contact the pharmacy team before attempting to order.

What happens after I submit a prescription order?

The information required for the order is reviewed before eligible prescription medicine is prepared for supply. Additional information may sometimes be required before processing can continue.

Can Top Pharma recommend a medicine for my symptoms?

Website information can help customers understand medicines and treatment categories, but it should not replace professional diagnosis or personalised medical advice. Speak with an appropriate healthcare professional if you need help choosing treatment.
