About Top Pharma

Top Pharma brings medicine reference links and practical appointment questions into one quiet, easy-to-browse directory. Start with a name or health topic and follow the resource that fits your question.

What this website provides

Our guides help you locate patient information, while topic pages organise related resources. Prescription and delivery pages focus on questions to take to the healthcare service looking after you.

Our editorial approach

We link to NHS medicine information and the electronic Medicines Compendium. Reference links are provided alongside each guide; they are separate services with their own editorial processes. Top Pharma does not claim a clinical review of its pages.

Service scope

This website does not dispense medicines, assess prescriptions or provide personalised medical advice. It does not claim pharmacy registration or clinical accreditation.
