FAQs about anxiety, panic & seizure medicines

What are anxiety medicines used for?

Anxiety medicines may be prescribed to help manage certain anxiety disorders and related symptoms. The choice of medicine depends on the individual's diagnosis and medical circumstances.

Can anxiety and panic disorder be treated with medication?

Medication may form part of a treatment plan for some people with anxiety or panic disorder. A healthcare professional can determine whether medication is appropriate and which treatment may be suitable.

What are seizure medicines used for?

Seizure medicines, often referred to as anti-seizure or anticonvulsant medicines, may be prescribed to help control certain types of seizures. The appropriate medicine depends on the type and cause of the seizures.

Can I stop taking seizure medicine when I feel better?

You should not stop a prescribed seizure medicine without speaking to your doctor. Suddenly changing or stopping some medicines can create serious risks.

Can these medicines cause side effects?

Yes. Side effects vary depending on the specific medicine and individual. Always read the medicine's information leaflet and consult your doctor or pharmacist if you experience unexpected or concerning symptoms.

Do I need a prescription for anxiety or seizure medicines?

Many medicines used for anxiety, panic disorders, and seizures are prescription-only medicines. Availability and prescription requirements depend on the specific medicine and applicable UK regulations.
